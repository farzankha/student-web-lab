Why Character References Are Useful

  • They allow special characters to be displayed correctly in HTML.
  • They are useful for reserved characters such as < and >.
  • They help display mathematical and scientific symbols.
  • They can represent copyright and trademark symbols.
  • They improve the readability and compatibility of webpages.
