❝ Quotation Marks & Apostrophes

Straight quotes (" and ') are ambiguous and look unprofessional in prose. Use the typographically correct curly quotes.

“Knowledge is power.” — Sir Francis Bacon
She said, ‘It’s time to code.’
The professor’s lecture was inspiring.

〈 Angle Brackets & Comparison

The less-than (<) and greater-than (>) signs are the most important entities in HTML — because a raw < would be misinterpreted by the browser as the start of an HTML tag.

The tag <p> marks a paragraph.
Use < and > for comparison: 5 < 10 and 10 > 5.
Encode an ampersand as &amp;.
