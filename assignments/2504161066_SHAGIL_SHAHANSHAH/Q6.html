About the Department

The Department of Computer Science at the University of Technology has been a hub of computing education and research since 1998. We offer undergraduate and postgraduate programmes in software engineering, artificial intelligence, data science, and computer networks, with a strong emphasis on hands-on learning.

Our faculty members are active researchers and industry practitioners who mentor students in project work, internships, and competitions.
