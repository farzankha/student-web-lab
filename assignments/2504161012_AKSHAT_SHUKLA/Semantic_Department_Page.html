About the Department

The Department of Computer Science and Information Technology at KMC Language University, Lucknow offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs including BCA and MCA. The department focuses on building strong foundations in programming, data structures, computer networks, and emerging technologies, preparing students for careers in the IT industry and higher studies.
