HTML Character Entities

Demonstration of special characters and HTML entities.

Mathematical Symbols

Plus or Minus: ±

Less than or equal to: ≤

Greater than or equal to: ≥

Not equal to: ≠

Multiplication: ×

Division: ÷

Currency Symbols

Indian Rupee: ₹

Dollar: $

Euro: €

Pound: £

Yen: ¥

Copyright and Trademark

Copyright: ©

Registered Trademark: ®

Trademark: ™

Quotation Marks

Left quotation mark: “

Right quotation mark: ”

Single quotation: ‘Hello’

Angle Brackets

Less than symbol: <

Greater than symbol: >

Example: <html>

Other Useful Characters

Ampersand: &

Non-breaking space: Hello World

Vertical bar: |

Section symbol: §

Paragraph symbol: ¶
