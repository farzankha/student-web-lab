Why Tables Are Appropriate Here

A timetable is inherently grid-shaped data: it maps a two-dimensional relationship between days (rows) and time periods (columns), where each cell holds related facts (subject + faculty). The <table> element expresses this row/column relationship semantically, allowing screen readers to announce "Monday, 9:00-10:00, Data Structures" correctly. rowspan merges the recess column vertically since it is identical every day, and colspan merges the Friday seminar slot across two periods since one activity spans that time - both keep the grid accurate without repeating data.
